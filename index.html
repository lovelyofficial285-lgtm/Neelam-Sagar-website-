10 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which cities do you work in?

Delhi and Mumbai are home bases. Neelam also travels for destination weddings and shoots, including Dubai, Thailand and Bali.

How early should I book bridal hair?

For wedding season, book 2 to 3 months ahead. For shorter notice, message on WhatsApp.

Do you offer hair extensions?

Yes. Extensions are fitted, blended and styled to match your hair.

Do you teach hairstyling classes?

Yes. Beginner and advanced classes, plus 1-on-1 sessions.

Is a trial available before the wedding?

Yes, trials are available for bridal and event bookings.
