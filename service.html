FAQ

Service questions.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the service, number of people, location and date. Share your details and Neelam will send a clear quote.

Do you travel for bookings?

Yes. Neelam works across Delhi and Mumbai and travels worldwide. Travel and stay are arranged with the booking.

Is a trial included for bridal hair?

Trials are available for bridal and event bookings, so your look is finalised before the day.

Can you style the whole bridal party?

Yes. Share the number of people and timings, and Neelam will plan the schedule.

How far in advance should I book?

For wedding season, 2 to 3 months ahead. For shorter notice, message on WhatsApp to check availability.

How do I confirm my booking?

After you share your details and agree the quote, the date is confirmed with an advance. Neelam will explain the steps.
